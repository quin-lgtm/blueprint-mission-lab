# Blueprint of Water — Mission Lab Art Direction

This page is the moral center of Blueprint of Water. It should not behave like a product feature page. It should feel like a quiet cinematic argument for water stewardship.

Core image: water is the narrator. A thin living stream underlines `Mission`, spills into a waterfall, pools, and becomes a river that carries invisible chemistry through subtle water-treatment and energy systems.

Visual priorities:
- Dark editorial science, not neon sci-fi.
- Water as sacred infrastructure, not decoration.
- Cyan = water/light/clarity.
- Gold = mineral trace/energy/responsibility.
- Chemistry labels belong inside the current.
- Text remains stable, calm, and readable.

Avoid:
- Cartoon water.
- Generic circuit-board lines.
- Clip-art treatment equipment.
- Over-animated typography.
- Canvas-only text.
